1️⃣ Core Foundations of Strong Poker Play

A good poker player:
	•	Plays tight early, wider late
	•	Understands position advantage
	•	Thinks in ranges, not hands
	•	Maximizes value when ahead
	•	Minimizes losses when behind
	•	Adjusts to opponents dynamically
	•	Controls pot size intentionally
	•	Understands fold equity

Bots must not “play cards.”
They must play ranges + EV + position + opponent tendencies.

⸻

2️⃣ Mathematical Layer (Essential for Bots)

🧮 Pot Odds

If:
	•	Pot = 100
	•	Opponent bets 50
	•	You must call 50 to win 150

You need 33% equity to call.
Implied Odds

Even if current pot odds are insufficient,
future potential payout can justify call.

Bots can simulate:
	•	Probability of hitting draw
	•	Estimated future opponent stack commitment

⸻

🧮 Equity Realization

Strong players:
	•	Don’t just calculate equity
	•	They consider whether they can realize it

Example:
	•	Weak draw out of position vs aggressive player
	•	Equity exists but hard to realize

Bluff only if EV positive.

3️⃣ Bluffing (Deep Tactical Breakdown)

Bluffing isn’t random aggression.

🎭 Types of Bluffs

1. Pure Bluff

No showdown value.

Example:
Board: A♠ K♠ 7♦ 2♣ 9♥
You have 6♣ 5♦

Only bet if:
	•	You represent credible range
	•	Opponent folds often

⸻

2. Semi-Bluff

Draw with equity.

Example:
Flush draw or straight draw.

Best bluff type because:
	•	You win if opponent folds
	•	You still win if you hit

Bots should prioritize semi-bluffs > pure bluffs.

⸻

3. Blocker Bluff

Holding key card reduces opponent’s strong combos.

Example:
You hold A♠
Board has 3 spades.
You block nut flush.

Bot logic:
	•	Count opponent nut combos
	•	Reduce bluff frequency if blockers absent

⸻

4. Polarized Bluff

Large bet representing nuts or nothing.

Used when:
	•	Range advantage
	•	Nut advantage
	•	River

⸻

🧠 When NOT to Bluff
	•	Multiway pots
	•	Calling stations
	•	Low-stakes recreational players
	•	Wet boards where opponent range connects strongly
	•	When you lack range credibility

Bots must detect player archetype first.

⸻

4️⃣ Positional Strategy

Position = most powerful advantage.

Early Position
	•	Play tight
	•	Strong range only

Middle Position
	•	Slightly wider

Late Position (Button/Cutoff)
	•	Steal blinds
	•	Wider opening range
	•	More bluffs
