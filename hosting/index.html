X Poker — Ten of a Kind's Free Poker Strategy Game

X Poker is Ten of a Kind's simulated Texas Hold'em strategy game: play against AI opponents across five competitive circuits — Europe, the Americas, Asia-Pacific, World Frontiers, and the Indian Ocean — each built from ten themed kingdoms with their own forts and noble titles. Climb the live circuit leaderboards, earn Aura points and forts, and meet Renoir, the dealer who never folds. It's entertainment and skill-building only — no real-money gambling, wagering, or prizes, ever.
