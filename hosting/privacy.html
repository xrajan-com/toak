Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Overview

Ten of a Kind Poker is a simulated poker and mental exercise game. You can continue with an anonymous guest account or register with email or Google. We collect and store the minimum information needed to provide the account and gameplay features described below.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: a Firebase user ID for every successful sign-in. Registered accounts may also include an email address and display name/username. Anonymous guest accounts do not require a name or email.
  • Profile information (optional): a short bio, selected kingdom, and an optional avatar image you upload in the app. The avatar image is stored locally on your device unless you later choose to upload it.
  • Gameplay and progress data: campaign progress, achievements, aura points, match statistics, and leaderboard entries tied to your user ID.

How We Use Information

  • Provide core functionality such as authentication, saved progress, and leaderboards.
  • Maintain gameplay balance and progress tracking.
  • Improve stability and performance.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. If a registered account qualifies for a public leaderboard, its chosen display name and leaderboard/gameplay statistics (such as Aura, AUP, activity, matches played, and performance statistics) are publicly visible in the app and on our website. Email addresses and raw Firebase user IDs are never published on the leaderboard.

We also share data with service providers only as needed to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database storage).

These providers may process data on our behalf under their terms. You can review their policies here:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Privacy Policy

Guest Mode

Continue as Guest creates an anonymous Firebase account identifier so the app can establish a game session, but it does not require your name or email. Minimal gameplay or account metadata may be associated with that identifier. Some progress is also stored locally and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.

Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data while your account is active. You can delete your account from My Profile in the app or use the support route below. Local-only data can be deleted by clearing app storage.

Account Deletion

The in-app Delete Account action is the preferred route because it verifies the signed-in account without putting personal data in a URL. More information and a support route are available at:

  • Account Deletion Request

Security

We use industry-standard security measures and rely on Google Firebase for secure data handling. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

This app is intended for users aged 18+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact
