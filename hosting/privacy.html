Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Overview

Ten of a Kind Poker is a simulated poker and mental exercise game. You can play as a guest without creating an account. If you choose to sign in, we collect and store the minimum information needed to provide the account and gameplay features described below.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (optional): email address, display name/username, and a Firebase user ID if you sign in with email or Google.
  • Profile information (optional): a short bio, selected kingdom, and an optional avatar image you upload in the app. The avatar image is stored locally on your device unless you later choose to upload it.
  • Gameplay and progress data: campaign progress, achievements, aura points, match statistics, and leaderboard entries tied to your user ID.

How We Use Information

  • Provide core functionality such as authentication, saved progress, and leaderboards.
  • Maintain gameplay balance and progress tracking.
  • Improve stability and performance.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with service providers only as needed to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (authentication and database storage).

These providers may process data on our behalf under their terms. You can review their policies here:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Privacy Policy

Guest Mode

If you play as a guest, account data is not created. Some progress may be stored locally on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.

Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data while your account is active. You can request deletion by contacting us. Local-only data can be deleted by clearing app storage.

Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at:

  • Account Deletion Request

Security

We use industry-standard security measures and rely on Google Firebase for secure data handling. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

This app is intended for users aged 18+ and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
