REAL TOURNAMENT POKER
Every match is a Texas Hold'em single-table tournament in the sit and go style. Blinds rise, stacks shrink and the top finishers get paid.
• No-limit Texas Hold'em betting: check, call, raise or move all-in
• Fort tables seat 6 or 8 players; main events seat a full ring of 10
• Rising blind levels that force real decisions
• Bubble play, short stacks and final-table pressure that feel like a live tournament
